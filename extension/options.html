Worthwhile settings

The token you were given. Filtering starts once this is set.

In your own words: what you want more of, and what to hide. The model scores every video against this.

What to hide

Hides clickbait, ragebait, drama and slop. Everything else stays where YouTube put it.

Also hides videos that are neither about your interests nor substantial.

Changing these doesn't re-score anything, so it takes effect straight away.

Shorts

Shorts titles are too thin to judge, and the shelf is where most of the slop is.

Each Short is judged from its thumbnail, title, channel and description before YouTube shows it. In testing, 8 removed none of 40 good Shorts; 6 removed about 1 in 6. The first few Shorts after opening YouTube fresh can't be filtered. Nothing is done to your YouTube account.

Shows a small "Worthwhile: bait" label on Shorts that fail your filter but weren't removed.

    Collect Shorts (testing)

    While this is on, every Short you swipe to is recorded on this device (ID, title, channel), with a check of whether YouTube had already queued it. Copy them as CSV, then fill in my_verdict (show or hide).
