Worthwhile settings

The token you were given. Filtering starts once this is set.

In your own words: what you want more of, and what to hide. The model scores every video against this.

What to hide

Hides clickbait, ragebait, drama and slop. Everything else stays where YouTube put it.

Also hides videos that are neither about your interests nor substantial.

Changing these doesn't re-score anything, so it takes effect straight away.

Shorts

Shorts titles are too thin to judge, and the shelf is where most of the slop is.

Shows a small "Worthwhile: bait" label on Shorts that fail your filter. Nothing is skipped.

Hidden channels

Channels you hid with "Hide channel". Their videos are hidden without being scored. Changes apply straight away.
